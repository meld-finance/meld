Everything in one place

Accounts & net worth

Banks, cards, brokerages, loans and mortgages, plus your home, vehicles and life insurance cash value, with a daily history.

Spending & budgets

Cash flow without double counting card payments, your own category rules, monthly budgets, and what changed against a year ago.

Recurring & autopay

Finds subscriptions, bills and paychecks, checks every autopay is covered by its bank account, and alerts you before it isn't.

Investments

Time-weighted performance, asset allocation, what's inside your funds, dividend income, and cost basis for every holding.

Cash & emergency fund

Months of runway from your real spending, what each account is earning, and nudges to move idle cash somewhere better.

Retirement

A projection in today's dollars with Social Security, property sales and rent, the gap to close, and what-ifs.

Tax

This year's federal and California estimate from your real income and trades, with tax-loss harvesting, Roth and 401(k) suggestions.

Insurance & estate

Life and disability coverage read from your policy PDFs, beneficiary checks on every account, and a packet for your family.

Ask Meld

Chat about your own numbers with Claude, or a local model through Ollama. It can read your data but never change it.

How each feature works →

Private by design

Meld runs on your computer. There's no Meld server, no account with the author, no analytics and no telemetry. Your data lives in a database on your machine, and the secrets in it are encrypted.

PlaidYour own keys. You sign in to your bank in Plaid's window, never Meld's; Meld receives balances, transactions, holdings and loan details.
Anthropic (optional)With a Claude model, your question and the data it looks up. Account, card and policy numbers are masked first.
Ollama (optional)The same chat with a local model, so nothing leaves your machine.
Yahoo FinanceTicker symbols only, for daily prices.
Your phoneOver Tailscale, a private network of only your own devices. Meld never opens itself to the internet.

Exactly what goes where →

Get started

You need your own Plaid developer account: Meld has no server of its own, so it connects to your banks with your keys. Sandbox is free and instant. Real accounts need Plaid to approve production access; the free Trial plan covers up to 10 bank logins.

Download Meld

Get the .dmg for Apple silicon or Intel and drag Meld to Applications. The first time, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Set it up

Choose a passphrase and paste your Plaid keys. Add an Anthropic key or Ollama to turn on Ask Meld (optional).

Try Sandbox, then go live

Look around with fake data (user_good / pass_good), then connect your real accounts.

Always on, on your phone

The Mac app is reachable from your phone over Tailscale while your Mac is awake: turn on phone access, scan the QR code and add Meld to your home screen.

For an always-on setup, run Meld with Docker on a home server, NAS or any Linux box. It listens on localhost only; reach it from your phone with tailscale serve.

git clone https://github.com/shimizust/meld.git
cd meld
./scripts/setup.sh
docker compose up -d --build
# open http://localhost:8080
