Ask Meld

An AI that actually knows your numbers

Ask in plain English. Meld looks up the accounts, transactions, holdings and plans it needs, and answers with specifics, not generic advice.

  1. Reads, never writes. It has read-only tools, so it can't move money or change your data.
  2. Account numbers are masked before anything is sent.
  3. Your choice of model: Claude for the best answers, or a free local model through Ollama so nothing leaves your Mac.

The honest trade-offs

You set up your own free Plaid developer account, and Plaid has to approve it before real banks connect. There's no app in the App Store yet: it's a Mac app you reach from your phone over a private network, or a Docker server you run. It's built for one household.

Why it's worth it

Holding your own Plaid keys is the point: no company sits between you and your bank, and there's no account database to leak. When Mint shut down, its users had to start over somewhere else. Meld's history is a database on your Mac that no company can switch off.

What it really costs

Meld itself is free. The only bills are for services you choose to use, paid directly to them, with no markup and no middleman.

Plaid's free Trial plan is for Plaid teams created after April 15, 2026; older teams can use Limited Production. Plaid sets its own prices; docs/plaid.md walks through it.

Get started

Try it with Plaid's test bank in a few minutes, then connect your real accounts once Plaid approves your keys.

Download Meld

Get the .dmg for Apple silicon or Intel and drag Meld to Applications. The first time, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Set it up

Choose a passphrase and paste your Plaid keys. Add an Anthropic key or Ollama to turn on Ask Meld (optional).

Try Sandbox, then go live

Look around with test data (user_good / pass_good), then connect your real accounts.

On your phone, or always on

Reach the Mac app from your phone over Tailscale while your Mac is awake: turn on phone access, scan the QR code and add Meld to your home screen.

For an always-on setup, run Meld with Docker on a home server, NAS or any Linux box. It listens on localhost only; reach it from your phone with tailscale serve.

git clone https://github.com/shimizust/meld-finance.git
cd meld-finance
./scripts/setup.sh
docker compose up -d --build
# open http://localhost:8080
