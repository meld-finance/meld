The app at a glance Guides

What each part of Meld does and how it works. For installing, see Install. Each area below has its own guide with screenshots.

Not financial advice. Meld's estimates and suggestions (tax, retirement, estate, Ask Meld) are for planning and education, not professional financial, tax or legal advice.

The app at a glance#

Five tabs:

  • Home — net worth, tasks that need attention, and what's coming up
  • Ask Meld — AI chat about your money and how Meld works
  • Accounts — linked banks, Cash, and Investments
  • Spending — Overview, Transactions, Recurring, Budgets
  • Plan — Retirement, Tax, Insurance, Estate, Profile
